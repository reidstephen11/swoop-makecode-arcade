Make it yours

Right now it's my game. Two changes and it's yours.

  1. In the green on start block, find set player to sprite. There's a tiny bucket sitting in it.
  2. Click the tiny bucket. A drawing grid opens.
  3. Draw whatever you want to catch with. Then hit the green Done button, bottom right.
  4. Back on the blocks, find set background color to. Click the coloured circle and pick a new sky.
You'll know it workedPress ▶. Your thing is down the bottom, and the sky has changed.
Watch outThe crow is white. Pick a dark sky or you won't see it coming.
Finished early?Use the whole 16×16 grid. Give it a face.

2

Fix the difficulty

The game is dull. Two numbers are wrong. You're going to fix them.

  1. Look at the very top of on start. The first two red blocks. That's all you need today.
  2. dropEvery is the wait between shiny things, in milliseconds. 2500 means one every 2.5 seconds — far too slow.
  3. fallSpeed is how fast they drop. 15 is a slow drift.
  4. Change both. Press ▶. Change them again. Keep going until it feels right.
Stuck for numbers? Try 700 and 60 — then beat your own score.
You'll know it workedYou're busy. If you can stand still and still catch things, it's still too easy.
Watch outChange one number at a time, or you won't know which one did it.
Finished early?Break it on purpose. What's the smallest dropEvery that's still playable?

3

Win the season

Right now you can only lose. Give the player a way to win.

  1. Click Info in the toolbox. Drag out on score 100 and drop it on an empty part of the workspace.
  2. Click the 100 and type 15.
  3. Click Game. Drag game over inside the block you just made.
  4. Check it says WIN, not LOSE. Click the word to swap it.
Two blocks, two drawers — pink from Info, purple from Game.
You'll know it workedCatch 15 things and the screen says YOU WIN!
Watch outThe purple block must sit inside the pink one, not underneath it.
Finished early?Tune it. Change 15 until winning takes about a minute.

4

Add a second crow

One crow isn't scary. You don't have to build a new one — copy the one that's already there.

  1. Find the purple on game update every 3500 ms stack — the one with crow all the way through it.
  2. Right-click the purple bar at the very top. Choose Duplicate.
  3. The copy sticks to your mouse. Click an empty spot to drop it.
  4. Change the copy's 3500 to something different. Try 2000.
Right-click the top bar, not a block inside it — or you'll only copy one line.
You'll know it workedTwo crows, arriving at different times. Noticeably harder.
Watch outSend a crow from the right and it flies backwards — the picture only faces one way.
Finished early?In the copy, change x to 166 and vx to -72. Now fix the backwards bird.

5

Your twist

It's your game now. Pick one of these — or ignore all four and do your own.

  1. A fifth shiny thing. In on start, find the tall orange array of block holding the four pictures. Click the ⊕ at its bottom, then draw into the empty square that appears.
  2. A bigger bucket. Sprites → set scale to — the very last block in that drawer.
  3. A sound when you catch. Music → play sound, dropped inside the catch block. Change until done to in background.
  4. Beat the clock. Info → start countdown. Change 10 to 60.
Top three sit inside on start. The orange block on the left is the loot — its ⊕ is right at the bottom.
You'll know it workedIt does the thing you pictured in your head. If it doesn't — change one thing and press ▶ again. That's the whole job.
Going furtherA helmet you can catch that blocks one swoop. A boss crow twice the size. Three birds. A high-score name. Put your hand up if you want help finding a block.

STUCK?

Read this first

Nearly every problem today is one of these four.

  • Can't find a block? Hunt by colour, not by name. Every block is the colour of the drawer it came out of. →
  • Nothing happened? Check the block is actually joined on — dragged until it clicks. A block floating on its own does nothing.
  • Broke it? Ctrl + Z undoes it. Press it as many times as you need.
  • Really broke it? Nothing you do here can wreck anything. Hand up and we'll put it back.
The one habit worth taking homeChange one thing. Press ▶. Look at what happened. Then change the next thing. Changing five things at once is how you end up lost.
