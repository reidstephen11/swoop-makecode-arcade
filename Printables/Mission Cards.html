Make it yours

Make one visible change, then try it out.

  1. Near the top of green on start, find the sky colour and set player to sprite.
  2. Choose the bucket OR the sky. Click its picture or coloured circle.
  3. For the bucket, recolour a few pixels. Keep some solid pixels so it can catch. Click Done.
  4. Restart the game. Can you see your change and catch a shiny thing? Try the other edit if you like.
You'll know it workedYou can point to your change and say what you chose.
Watch outTest that you can see the crow and catcher against your chosen sky.
Try a challengeDraw a new catcher. Does its shape change how it catches?

2

Find your fun

Choose your game's feel: relaxed, busy or tricky.

  1. Find the first two red variable blocks in on start. Choose one setting to change.
  2. dropEvery: smaller means more frequent loot. Try 700 instead of 2500 as one experiment.
  3. fallSpeed: larger means faster falling. Try 60 instead of 15 as another experiment.
  4. Predict what will happen. Change one number, restart and test for 20 seconds. Keep it or adjust it.
These are starting settings. Your design can be relaxed, busy or tricky.

Watch outChange one number at a time, or you won't know which one did it.

3

Give it a win

Right now you can only lose. Give the player a way to win.

  1. Click Info in the toolbox. Drag out on score 100 and drop it on an empty part of the workspace.
  2. Change 100 to 2 for a quick test. This rounded-top event stands on its own.
  3. Click Game. Drag game over inside the block you just made.
  4. Select WIN. Restart and catch 2 things. Then choose your real target, such as 15.
Two blocks, two drawers - pink from Info, purple from Game.
You'll know it workedReach your target and see YOU WIN! Restore your chosen target after testing.
Watch outThe purple block must sit inside the pink one, not underneath it.
Try a challengeCan a partner win in 30-60 seconds? Use their feedback to tune it.

4

Change the challenge

Want more crows? Reuse the event and choose when they arrive.

  1. Find the purple on game update every 3500 ms stack - the one with crow all the way through it.
  2. Right-click the purple bar at the very top. Choose Duplicate.
  3. The copy sticks to your mouse. Click an empty spot to drop it.
  4. Change the copy's 3500 to something different. Try 2000.
Right-click the top bar, not a block inside it - or you'll only copy one line.
You'll know it workedAn extra stream of crows arrives. Is the game still fair and fun?
Watch outCopy the whole event. Ask your teacher to show the laptop's right-click gesture.
Try a challengeChange the timing or flight path. Can you make more crows feel fairer?

Your twist

Choose ONE idea. Decide what you expect, then test it.

  1. Fifth item: in on start, find the loot array. Click + at its bottom; draw in the new square. Test until your item falls.
  2. Bigger bucket: from Sprites, put set scale to in on start, below player creation. Select player and 1.5. Restart: is it bigger?
  3. Catch sound: from Music, put play sound inside the existing Player-Shiny overlap event. Choose a sound and in background. Catch to hear it.
  4. Timer: from Info, put start countdown 60 in on start. Watch it count down. Reach your win target before zero, or you lose.

STUCK?

Read this first

Find the block, check its event, test again.

  • Can't find a block? Use its name, colour and shape. Try toolbox search too.
  • Where does it go? Action blocks go inside an event. Rounded-top events, such as on start and on score, stand on their own.
  • Unexpected result? Undo your last change with Ctrl + Z (Mac: Cmd + Z). Restart and test.
  • Catcher disappeared? Open its picture and draw solid pixels. Still stuck? Ask for a working checkpoint.
The one habit worth taking homePredict. Change one thing. Restart. Notice what happened. Decide what to try next.
